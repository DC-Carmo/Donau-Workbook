The RDA method

Train like the pros

See the game. Then play it.

Camp isn't just drills. Players break down attack and defence on the RDA Tactical Board — the same tool coaches use to build and animate plays — then take it onto the grass. Strength, speed and testing round out a genuinely modern development week.

Every player leaves with an individual report on their development.
